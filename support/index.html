Getting Started

Simple Blood Pressure helps you record and review your own blood pressure entries. The app is intended for personal tracking and informational use only.

Important: Simple Blood Pressure is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency guidance, or clinical interpretation. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns or medical decisions.

How to Use the App

How do I add a blood pressure reading?

Open the app and use the option to add a new reading. Enter your systolic pressure, diastolic pressure, heart rate if available, and any notes you want to remember. Save the entry when finished.

What do systolic and diastolic mean?

Systolic is the top number in a blood pressure reading. Diastolic is the bottom number. The app lets you record these values for personal tracking, but it does not interpret them as medical advice.

Can I add notes or symptoms?

Yes. If the app provides a notes field, you can use it to record context such as how you felt, what you were doing, or other details you want to remember.

How do I review previous readings?

Open the app and view your saved entries. Depending on the app version, your readings may be shown in a list, summary, or trend view.

Can I edit or delete an entry?

If editing or deleting is available in your app version, select the saved entry and choose the edit or delete option. Deleted entries may not be recoverable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Simple Blood Pressure a medical device?

No. Simple Blood Pressure is not a medical device. It is intended only for personal tracking and informational purposes.

Does the app diagnose high or low blood pressure?

No. The app does not diagnose, treat, monitor, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional if you have questions about your readings.

Should I use the app in an emergency?

No. Do not rely on the app in an emergency. If you believe you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately or seek urgent medical attention.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, your data may also be stored in your private iCloud account for syncing and backup.

Can Simple Blood Pressure see my health data?

No. The app is designed so your blood pressure and health-related data remain private. We do not operate cloud servers for storing your blood pressure data and do not have access to your private iCloud data.

Does the app use analytics or tracking?

No. Simple Blood Pressure does not use analytics, tracking tools, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics services.

How do I back up my data?

If iCloud sync or device backup is enabled on your Apple device, your data may be included in your private Apple-managed backups or syncing. You are responsible for maintaining access to your device, Apple ID, iCloud account, and backups.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete entries in the app if deletion is available, or remove the app from your device. If your data is synced with iCloud, you may also need to delete associated iCloud data through your Apple device settings.

Why are my readings not syncing?

Check that iCloud is enabled on your device, that you are signed in with the correct Apple ID, that your device has an internet connection, and that iCloud has enough available storage. Syncing is handled by Apple iCloud and may be affected by your device or account settings.

Can I use the app without iCloud?

Yes, the app can be used for local tracking on your device. Without iCloud, syncing between devices may not be available.

Troubleshooting

The app is not saving my entry. What should I try?
  1. Make sure all required fields are filled in correctly.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Restart your device.
  4. Check that your device has available storage.
  5. Install the latest app and iOS updates when available.
The app looks incorrect or behaves unexpectedly. What should I do?
  1. Close and reopen the app.
  2. Restart your device.
  3. Check for iOS updates.
  4. Check for an updated version of Simple Blood Pressure in the App Store.
I lost my data. Can you recover it?

No. We do not have access to your local device data or private iCloud data, so we cannot recover deleted, lost, or corrupted entries for you.
