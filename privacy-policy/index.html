Simple Blood Pressure

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is extremely important to us. Simple Blood Pressure is designed so that your blood pressure and health-related data remain private, under your control, and inaccessible to us.

Effective Date May 23, 2026
App Name Simple Blood Pressure

1. Overview

Simple Blood Pressure helps you record and manage blood pressure-related information. The app is built with a privacy-first approach.

We do not collect, access, sell, share, or transmit your personal health data to our servers.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or have access to:

  • Blood pressure readings
  • Heart rate entries
  • Notes or symptoms you enter
  • Medication information
  • Health history
  • Personal identifiers linked to your health data
  • Analytics tied to your health records
  • Any other data you enter into the app

Your data stays private and is not visible to us.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored locally on your device.

If you choose to use Apple iCloud, your data may also be stored in your own private iCloud account for syncing and backup purposes. This iCloud storage is controlled by you and governed by Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy practices.

We do not operate cloud servers for storing your blood pressure data, and we do not have access to your private iCloud data.

4. iCloud Sync

If iCloud sync is enabled, your app data may sync between your Apple devices using your personal iCloud account.

We cannot view, retrieve, modify, or delete your iCloud data. Access to iCloud data is controlled by your Apple ID, device settings, and Apple’s security systems.

You can manage iCloud access at any time through your device settings.

5. No Analytics or Tracking

Simple Blood Pressure does not use analytics, tracking tools, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics services.

We do not track how you use the app.

6. No Sale or Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or health data with third parties.

Because we do not have access to your blood pressure data, we cannot use it for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing.

7. Apple Health

Simple Blood Pressure does not currently integrate with Apple Health.

If Apple Health integration is added in the future, the app will only access or write Apple Health data with your explicit permission through Apple’s permission system. You will be able to manage or revoke those permissions through your device settings.

8. Permissions

The app may request permissions only when needed for app functionality, such as access to iCloud.

You can allow, deny, or revoke permissions at any time through your device settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended for use by children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Security

Because your health data is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud account, protecting your device and Apple ID is important.

We recommend that you use device passcodes, biometric authentication, two-factor authentication for your Apple ID, and regular software updates.

11. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by deleting entries within the app or removing the app from your device.

If your data is synced with iCloud, you may also need to delete associated iCloud data through your Apple device settings.

We cannot delete your data for you because we do not have access to it.

12. Medical Disclaimer

Simple Blood Pressure is intended for personal tracking and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding blood pressure concerns, symptoms, medication, or medical decisions.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.
